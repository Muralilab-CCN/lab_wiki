# 🧠 EEG Data Acquisition Protocol

This protocol outlines the standard operating procedure for preparing a participant and acquiring clean EEG data in the Murali Lab.

---

## 📋 Pre-Session Checklist
- [ ] Workstation turned on and impedance check software running.
- [ ] EEG cap size selected based on participant's head circumference.
- [ ] Adequate supply of conductive gel and clean syringes.
- [ ] Sanitized cotton swabs and alcohol prep pads ready.

---

## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Procedure

### 1. Participant Preparation
1. Measure the participant's head circumference from the nasion to the inion to verify cap size.
2. Mark the **Cz** position using an erasable skin marker.
3. Clean the skin at electrode site locations using an alcohol prep pad to lower skin impedance.

!!! note "Pro Tip"
    Ensure the participant's hair is completely dry before applying the cap. Wet hair can cause electrical bridging between neighboring channels!

### 2. Cap Application & Gel Injection
* Place the cap on the participant's head, aligning **Cz** with your marked spot.
* Secure the chin strap comfortably.
* Use a blunt-tip syringe to push aside hair and inject a small amount of conductive gel into each electrode well.

!!! danger "Safety Warning"
    Never press too hard with the blunt-tip syringe. It should never cause pain or scratch the participant's skin.
